about=Tools to prepare the GML files required by the Spanish Cadastre electronic office: cadastral parcel GML (INSPIRE CadastralParcels 4.0) for graphic validation reports and building GML (INSPIRE BuildingExtended2D) for construction location reports (ICUC). Create GML files from your own layers with an assistant for the kind of change (segregation, division, aggregation, grouping, correction); download a single parcel, its neighbours and its buildings by cadastral reference; merge or split selected parcels (equal parts, percentages, areas, shares or strips) and merge several GML files; check a GML before uploading it (structure, XSD schema, identifiers, rings, areas, overlaps and gaps, reference system and UTM zone) and compare it with the current cadastral parcels (affected parcels, operation, ±1 cm outer boundary, land without parcel); convert parcel GML files from schema 3.0 to 4.0 or repair them; area and coordinates report (boundaries with neighbouring parcels, vertices and sides); adjust a survey to displaced or rotated cadastral mapping (transformation parameters and adjusted GML); compare two GML files (vertex displacement, areas, coordinate rounding); save an HTML check report. Covers the territory managed by the Spanish Directorate General for Cadastre; the foral cadastres (Navarre and the Basque Country) are detected and handled separately. This is NOT an official tool: always validate the result in the Sede Electrónica del Catastro. Cadastral data: Dirección General del Catastro. User interface in Spanish.
